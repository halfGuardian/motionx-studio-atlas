# Independent evidence axes and review boundaries

| Axis | Source-supported assertion | What it cannot establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Identity | Named directory member, legal/trading name, non-shared official domain, supported aliases | Current operation, parent ownership, branch identity or facility count |
| Geography | Country association, explicit municipality/region, sourced coordinate precision | A parent's country, a foreign branch, a city centroid as a studio entrance |
| Company services | Explicit animation/VFX/games or technical-service self-description | The same service at every branch or independent capability confirmation |
| Facility capabilities | Reviewed site-specific capability evidence with source/date | Blanket commercial availability or inferred capture from camera motion control, VFX or animation |
| Operation/history | Historical participation, self-reported status, source revision/absence | Automatic closure, relocation or acquisition confirmation |
| Rights | Precisely scoped licence, attribution, terms and collection-access decision | Permission for unrelated sources, personal contacts, prose, photographs or media |

These axes supplement Class A/B/C and preserve existing source history. An approximate company reference remains Class C, has a separate display layer, and does not write coordinates into its original record. Confirmed-only capability filters use the existing facility evidence; a company sector remains a source assertion. The original 40,376 source points retain their precision limitations.

Reconciliation uses stable source-specific IDs first, then operating-name/domain/country evidence. Changed source names, conflicting operating identities, shared domains, subsidiaries, fuzzy aliases and foreign geography remain review cases. Matching a company never creates another branch. Distinct facilities and operating brands retain separate existing IDs; no merges or parent relationships are applied by the global tool.

All admitted/replayed observations have one outcome and retain date, source URL, matching reason, confidence, original source rights and input hash. Repeated observations remain in the audit and point to the first source identity. They do not increase the distinct-business count. Historical event activities can be out of scope for this projection without removing an existing company. Generic production/agency entries require specialist-production qualification.

The unpinned-company audit covers every 1,677 company-only reference. Its dispositions describe the **available evidence**, including insufficient evidence, conflicts and historical assertions. This is not 1,677 newly independently researched operating locations. Only Floating Rock's city reference was newly source-reviewed for display. The 256 other recorded city/region associations remain ungeocoded pending individual evidence and licensing review. All 24 historical/status assertions require investigation; acquisition does not imply closure. The three potential duplicate/identity conflicts remain unresolved.

The four existing NZ facility dossiers remain pending, with their original evidence and no new geocodes or independent human approvals. None is converted to a confirmed capture facility by these tools. The fourteen official-page evaluation candidates are excluded from ingestion and remain unlabelled: no precision/recall or physical-facility verification may be inferred from software matching. Camera motion control and photographic slow-motion capture must not be mislabelled as skeletal/body/performance capture.

Maintainer review must consider the exact source-backed change in the draft PR. Exported dashboard decisions are unauthenticated working notes, bound to a report/content hash. They are not human approval, approval bundles or import authorisation. Existing content-bound publication safeguards remain in place. Required GitHub review enforcement is not established by this programme; draft state and passing CI alone do not enforce human approval.
